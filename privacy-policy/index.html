01

Introduction

Luma Gallery is an Android photo and video gallery application. It is designed to help you browse, organize, edit, share, analyze, and protect media stored on your device. This Privacy Policy describes the data handling of the current Luma Gallery application build identified by package name com.example.g.

Read this policy together with the permission prompts shown by Android and the privacy information of any service or app you choose when sharing content.

02

Information we collect

The application works with data stored on your device. Depending on the features you use, this can include selected photos and videos; media identifiers, file names, MIME types, dates, dimensions, album or folder information, and related metadata; local analysis results; favorites; app settings; and encrypted Private Vault metadata.

For app-lock and Private Vault verification, the application stores a salted verifier rather than your secret code in readable form. The app does not provide an account-registration feature in this build.

03

Photos and videos access

Luma Gallery asks Android for access to photos and videos so it can display, organize, edit, analyze, share, restore, and manage the media available to the app. On supported Android versions, you may grant access to selected visual media rather than the entire library. Android controls the permission decision, and you can change it in device settings.

When you place media in Private Vault, the app creates an encrypted copy in its app-private storage before the original is moved to Android's recycle bin or otherwise handled through the Android media flow. You should verify the result before permanently removing an original.

04

On-device AI/ML processing

Luma Gallery includes a bundled LiteRT/TensorFlow Lite model and local forensic-analysis components. Image analysis, metadata inspection, and stored analysis results are processed on your device in the current build. The source code contains no implemented upload path for media analysis.

Analysis is probabilistic and may be unavailable or incorrect. It is not proof of whether content is authentic, generated, edited, or safe. Do not rely on a result as the sole basis for an important decision.

05

Private Vault and encryption

Private Vault stores selected media in app-private storage and encrypts it using AES-256-GCM with a key managed by Android Keystore. Vault metadata is also encrypted. Temporary decrypted previews can be created in the app cache while the vault is open and are cleared when the vault closes or locks.

Encryption reduces risk but does not guarantee absolute security. A rooted or compromised device, malware, an unlocked device, screenshots, backups outside the app's control, or loss of the device may reduce protection. Keep your device and secret code secure.

06

Local storage and database

Luma Gallery uses local app storage, SharedPreferences, and a Room database for settings, favorites, analysis records, and feature state. The app's backup configuration excludes private and sensitive app data. These local records help provide gallery and analysis features; they are not an online user profile in the current build.

07

Network communication

The current source build has no implemented network client or remote media-analysis service. Its Android network security configuration disallows cleartext traffic. This does not prevent Android, a distribution store, your device manufacturer, or a separate app you choose from applying their own network practices.

If a future version adds remote processing, updates, analytics, crash reporting, or another online service, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

08

Data sharing and third parties

Luma Gallery does not include a built-in media-sharing service in the current source build. When you intentionally use Android's share sheet, the receiving app or service may receive the content and information you select. Its privacy policy then applies.

If you install the app from a third-party store, that store may process account, download, device, or diagnostic information under its own policy. This policy covers Luma Gallery's application behavior, not the store's separate practices.

09

Advertising and analytics

No advertising, behavioral analytics, or crash-reporting SDK is included in the reviewed application dependencies. This privacy-policy webpage also contains no analytics or advertising script. If that changes in a future release, Luma Gallery will update this policy and the applicable store disclosures.

11

Data retention and deletion

Local app data is retained on your device while it is needed for the feature that created it or until you clear it, remove the related media, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can permanently remove app-private Private Vault media and local records. Luma Gallery does not provide cloud backup or recovery in the current build.

Media moved to Android's recycle bin is subject to Android and device storage behavior. Before clearing data or uninstalling, restore any Private Vault items you want to keep.

12

Children's privacy

Luma Gallery is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app can display media already stored on a device, a parent or guardian should supervise use where appropriate. If you believe device content requires removal, use Android and app storage controls to remove it.

13

Data security

The app uses Android permission controls, app-private storage, Android Keystore-backed encryption for Private Vault content, encrypted vault metadata, release code shrinking, and cleartext-traffic restrictions. Security measures reduce risk but cannot eliminate it. Keep Android updated and avoid installing software from untrusted sources.

14

Your choices and rights

You can control media and notification permissions in Android settings; remove individual media from the app's available library; manage or restore vault content; clear application storage; and uninstall the app. Rights under privacy law vary by location and circumstances. This offline-first build does not operate a Luma Gallery account service, so there is no online account dashboard.

15

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the application or its data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Review the policy after updating the app, especially if a release introduces an online or third-party service.

16

Contact information

A company name, postal address, and dedicated privacy email have not been published for the current project, so they are not invented here. For project information, use the Luma Gallery project repository or the publisher contact details shown in the store listing from which you obtained the app.
